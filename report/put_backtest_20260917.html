方法与局限Method and limitations

权利金用 Black-Scholes 近似: 波动率 = (HV20+HV60)/2 × VRP, 行权价由目标 Delta 反解; 入场=信号后首个交易日收盘, 持有到期按收盘结算; ROC = (权利金 − 到期内在价值) / 行权价 (现金担保)。没有历史 IV/买卖价差/财报日期: 真实交易中财报前 IV 更高、跳空风险更大, 回测不包含这些。绝对 ROC 取决于 VRP 假设, 请重点看组间差异 (击穿率、同日超额、CVaR)。Premium via Black-Scholes: vol = (HV20+HV60)/2 × VRP, strike solved from target delta; entry = close of first trading day after signal, cash-settled at expiry close; ROC = (premium − intrinsic at expiry) / strike (cash secured). No historical IV, spreads or earnings dates. Absolute ROC depends on the VRP assumption; focus on differences between groups (breach rate, same-date excess, CVaR).
